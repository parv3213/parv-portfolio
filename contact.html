§ 04.2 / How to write

A brief that gets a same-day reply.

  1. What you're trying to do.One sentence. The outcome, not the stack.
  2. The constraint that's biting.Deadline, headcount, audit date, budget — whichever is the blocker.
  3. What good looks like in four weeks.Two bullets. The state you want to be in.
  4. One artifact.Repo link, spec doc, or a rough Loom. Context over poetry.
