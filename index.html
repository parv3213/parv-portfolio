Architecting USDs V2, then closing the Quantstamp batch.

Joined Sperax in late 2023 onto V2 — the clean-slate, state-based rebuild of an over-collateralized stablecoin with dripper/rebase economics, an oracle router, and Uniswap V3–based strategies. Owned the implementation end-to-end across token, vault, rebase, oracle, collateral, and strategy modules, with The Graph subgraphs and TypeScript/Go backends layered on top. Same tenure also covered Demeter (Uniswap V3 farms-as-a-service — Genesis partners GMX, PlutusDAO, Gains, Vela) and Chaquen (on-chain fantasy sports, ~27K users on Polygon).

The Quantstamp batch then hit the whole USDs repo: approvals, input validation, event emission, rebase/redeem edge cases — alongside gas work on storage layout, caching hot slots, and execution paths. CI grew solhint, gas-diff, coverage gates, and sequential-flow diagrams in-repo so reviewers could walk the state machine without reading bytecode.

A B2B video-and-email campaign platform, shipped end-to-end.

Boomerang is Tavolo's campaign product for restaurant marketing: media gallery, video uploads, email delivery, role-based workflows, and regional behavior. I owned it across Go, Node, and React — multi-step approvals, RBAC, collaborative editing, async notifications, and a first-class media path.

The core upgrade was swapping a Google-Drive-backed flow for direct-to-Azure-Blob uploads: chunked client sessions, concurrency control, fault-tolerant processing, SAS download URLs, cache-invalidation fixes, and gallery lifecycle cleanup. SendGrid became the email spine for video campaigns; batching and throughput passes pulled the send pipeline onto a single well-instrumented path. Also shipped AI-assisted customer segmentation (chat UI + CSV bulk import) that cut manual segmentation from hours to minutes.

An AI pipeline that runs my own career operations.

Five skill-scoped agents, one concern each: profile curator, recruiter-lens review, company discovery, job hunter, outreach drafting. The portfolio you're reading is a pure function of that database.
